## 1. Strategic Context

ACF Miami is the anchor of the entire 12-month engagement. **50% of the retainer focus flows toward this event.** It is TIACA's flagship biennial — the #1 tradeshow in air cargo by their own positioning. The 2026 target is 5,000 unique visitors (up from 3,500 in the prior cycle) with 220+ exhibitors and sponsors.

The current ACF presence is built on Cvent — a third-party event management tool that handles registration and logistics but is **not a conversion environment**. It communicates information; it does not sell the event. Sponsorship packages in the existing prospectus are functional but not premium sales tools.

**The problem this site solves**: ACF 2026 has real commercial scale ($7,500–$59,500 booth packages, pavilion at ~$39,750, plus sponsorship tiers) but the current digital presence does not reflect the value of those packages. Serious sponsors and exhibitors make six-figure event decisions — they need a site that makes ACF feel like the premium opportunity it actually is.

This site is not a replacement for Cvent's registration backend. It is a **dedicated premium sales and conversion environment** that sits in front of Cvent — the conversion hub that earns the click to register.

**Three audiences, three conversion paths**:
1. **Sponsors** → tier packages → inquiry form → Kenneth/Sandro follow-up
2. **Exhibitors** → booth packages → inquiry/booking → Sandro/MGME
3. **Attendees** → "why attend" → register on Cvent

## 4. Technical Approach

### Platform

**Recommended: Static site (React + Tailwind, Vite, deployed on Vercel or Netlify)**

Rationale:
- ACF site is a purpose-built event sales tool — no ongoing content editing required for most sections
- Speaker/program section: use a simple JSON data file that Riley/Rachel updates (or Sirak updates on their behalf)
- Maximum design flexibility, best performance, no CMS constraints
- Clean deployment: `acf2026.tiaca.org` or `aircargoforum2026.tiaca.org`
- Sirak dev team builds in 2–3 days on this stack

**Alternative: Webflow**
- If TIACA.org is also on Webflow, keeps the ecosystem unified
- CMS allows Riley to update speakers/agenda without Sirak involvement
- Slightly slower build (~4 days vs 2–3)

**Alternative: WordPress page on TIACA.org**
- Least recommended — existing WP theme constraints limit the premium conversion design required for this project
- Also conflates the main institutional site with the event sales funnel

**Decision gate**: Align with platform decision made for TIACA.org redesign to maintain consistency.

### URL Strategy
- **Preferred**: `acf2026.tiaca.org` or `aircargoforum2026.tiaca.org` (subdomains)
- **Fallback**: `tiaca.org/acf-2026` (subdirectory)
- **Not recommended**: external domain (loses TIACA.org domain authority)
- DNS coordination: Rachel / hosting provider

### Registration Integration
- Attendee registration: links to Cvent (Sandro/Riley provide the URL)
- Booth booking: links to Cvent floor plan selector (from prospectus)
- Sponsor inquiry: form → Sandro + Riley email
- Attendee/exhibitor interest capture: Sirak-built form → Riley's CRM / email list

### Analytics
- Google Analytics 4 — dedicated ACF 2026 property
- Conversion goals: Attend CTA click, Exhibit form submit, Sponsor form submit, Floor plan view
- UTM tracking for all inbound links (from TIACA.org, LinkedIn, email campaigns, TNN episodes)
- Heatmapping: Microsoft Clarity (free) for conversion optimization insights
- Monthly analytics review delivered as part of the performance review template (Deliverable 10)

## 9. Content Rules

- **Lead with scale and exclusivity**: 5,000 attendees, C/VP-level, #1 in air cargo. Every section earns its place by reinforcing this.
- **Three clear audiences, three clear paths**: The site must immediately send sponsors, exhibitors, and attendees down their own funnel. No generic pages.
- **Booth pricing is public**: Use confirmed pricing from the prospectus. Transparency builds trust with serious exhibitors.
- **Sponsorship as access, not logos**: Frame sponsorship as owning a room of decision-makers, not buying banner placement.
- **Anti-US sentiment**: Do not ignore it — pre-empt it. Miami as a world-class destination works in our favor if messaged correctly. Home city, logistical hub, entertainment infrastructure.
- **Exhibitor incentive**: The $500/50-registrant incentive is a strong behavioral hook — feature it prominently on the Exhibit page.
- **New audiences**: Students, supply-chain professionals, LATAM, India — the 5,000 target requires new channel expansion. The site should signal openness to these groups without alienating the senior professional core.
- **No Glyn personally**: The site's authority comes from the event's scale, not any individual's profile.
